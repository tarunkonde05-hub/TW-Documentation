\section{Multi-line equations}

\subsection{Centering \& alignment}

The \texttt{gather} environment centres every line:
\begin{gather}
    x = 5 \label{eq:x}\\
    x + y = 3 \label{eq:xy}
\end{gather}

The \texttt{align} environment lines them up at the \texttt{=} sign:
\begin{align}
    x     &= 5 \\
    x + y &= 3
\end{align}

From \eqref{eq:x} and \eqref{eq:xy}, $y = -2$.
